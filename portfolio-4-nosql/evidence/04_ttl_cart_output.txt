1. THE PROBLEM: ABANDONED SHOPPING CARTS IN HIGH-SCALE E-COMMERCE
======================================================================
In high-volume e-commerce platforms like EkoMart Online, approximately
70% of created shopping carts are abandoned without checkout.
In relational systems (e.g. PostgreSQL), purging millions of stale carts
requires scheduled batch DELETE queries that induce write-lock contention,
table bloat, and vacuuming overhead.
MongoDB provides native Time-To-Live (TTL) single-field indexes where a
low-priority background thread (ttlMonitor) automatically evicts expired
documents without application-level maintenance.
